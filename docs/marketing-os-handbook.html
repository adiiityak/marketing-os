LeadSquared Marketing OS Handbook

How the marketing AI OS fits together, what each part does, and how to set it up for a new teammate. Covers the Asset Library, Template Studio, the agents, the feedback loop that teaches Claude, the Content Repository and the Figma rules.

Owner Aditya KanojiyaUpdated 30 Oct 2026Plugin leadsquared-marketing-os 0.3.0Dashboard version 18
01 · Overview

What it is

The Marketing OS is a set of Claude skills, a shared dashboard and a few automations. Together they let the marketing team brief Claude in plain language and get on-brand work back: ads, landing pages, copy, webinar banners, event creatives, SEO briefs, sales decks and call insights. Everything Claude makes is filed in one library, rated by the team, and those ratings and comments steer the next piece of work.

It is built in three layers. Every agent works through them in this order.

Layer 1

Design system

Brand voice, colours, type, logo rules, channel specs and on-brand starting templates. Lives in the plugin's brand skills and in Template Studio.

Layer 2

Agents

One skill per task: web design, creative, PPC, copy, design production, demo intelligence, SEO briefs, sales decks, brand review, repository search, Template Studio.

Layer 3

System of record

The Asset Library dashboard (assets, ratings, feedback, team), the Content Repository index, and the Figma output project.

Who does what

People set the brief, pick options and approve. Claude does the volume work, files it, and learns from the team's ratings. Nothing is published, sent or uploaded outside without a person approving it.

02 · Architecture

How it works

A typical request moves through the system like this:

  1. BriefSomeone asks Claude in the LSQ OS project, or fills a template in Template Studio.
  2. Read firstThe agent loads the brand skills, the product marketing context, the top and bottom rated assets for that industry, and every team comment that applies.
  3. Make optionsIt produces 2–3 options, says which comments it applied, and asks the person to pick or edit.
  4. DeliverThe approved piece goes out as a file. Designs can also become an editable frame in the Figma output project.
  5. FileThe piece is filed in the Asset Library under the requester, industry and type.
  6. Rate and commentThe team rates it 1–5 and says why. Each comment is stored separately and fed into the next brief.

04 · Create page

Template Studio

Template Studio lets anyone, Figma user or not, make a design from the team's own templates. Pick a template, fill a short form, and the preview updates as you type.

Three ways to use a template

ModeWhat you getWho builds it
Use this templateSame layout with your content. Download a PNG (1x, 2x or 3x), push an editable copy to Figma, or save it to the library.The Studio, instantly
Make a V2A variation in the same family: new background, layout shift, accent or crop. Describe what should change.Claude, from the copied brief
Totally new templateA new design for the same size and channel, built from the brand rules.Claude, from the copied brief

Speaker photos are cut out automatically

The team's favourite style puts the speaker on the design with no photo background (Bambi Frazier square post). When you upload a photo to a template that uses that style, the Studio removes the background in your browser in a few seconds. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • A photo that is already a cut-out PNG is used as is.
  • Use original puts the photo back with its background.
  • Best results: a clear head-and-shoulders photo, one person, plain or blurred background.

Text that fits

Titles, names and designations shrink to fit their space. If a field is longer than the design expects, the form tells you, so you can shorten it rather than end up with tiny text.

Push to Figma (editable)

One click turns the preview into a real Figma frame that designers can tweak: every text is a text layer in Poppins or Inter, shapes are frames with their colours, strokes and corner radii, and photos are image fills. Cut-out speakers arrive as a photo with a mask, so the cut-out stays editable.

  • It lands in the matching file of the LSQ OS Generated Output project, on the page for the month (for example 2026-10), to the right of the existing frames.
  • The frame is named date · title · speaker · template id.
  • A progress bar shows the upload and build steps. It usually takes 15–60 seconds.
  • Each person needs the Figma connector connected in their own Claude settings, and approves the page using it the first time.

Download PNG

The PNG is drawn in the browser without any network call. You choose 1x, 2x or 3x width. If the browser blocks the save, the image opens on the page: right-click it (press and hold on a phone) and choose Save image.

05 · Learning loop

Feedback memory

Every comment the team leaves is kept and used. Updating a rating no longer replaces the old comment: each submission is stored as its own entry with the asset, template, industry, type, rating, note, who left it and when.

  1. Someone rates an asset and says why"Speaker photo too small, make the person bigger and keep the yellow CTA."
  2. The comment is loggedIt goes into the feedback collection, linked to the asset and, for Studio work, to the template it came from.
  3. The Studio shows itOpen that template and the panel Team feedback Claude applies lists the comments: first for this template, then this industry, then general creative notes.
  4. Briefs carry itEvery V2 or new-template brief includes those comments, newest first.
  5. Agents apply itBefore making anything, the agents read the log, apply what fits, and open their answer with the list of comments they applied. If a comment conflicts with the brand rules, the brand rules win and the agent says so.
  6. Chat feedback counts tooWhen you tell Claude in chat "too wordy" or "make the logo bigger", it saves that to the log as well.
  7. Weekly digestThe Monday sync summarises the week's comments as "What the team asked for this week".
Write comments Claude can act on

"It was more" or "sfs" can't be applied. Good comments name the thing and the change: "title too long, keep it under two lines", "use a darker background for the healthcare panel", "the stat-led headline got more clicks than the question headline".

Brand and specialist skills

leadsquared-voice-and-tone (copy always), leadsquared-visual-guideline (colour tokens, web components, accessibility) and leadsquared-design-system (logo, typeface by channel, ad specs). Where the two visual skills disagree, the visual guideline wins on colours and components; the design system wins on logo, type by channel and ad specs. The specialist marketing skills (ad-creative, ads, copywriting, copy-editing, cro, emails, cold-email, content-strategy, lead-magnets, marketing-ideas, onboarding, product-marketing, social, image) are used by the agents where they fit.

Rules every agent follows

  • Claims: only numbers marked [V] in the claims ledger are used freely; [I] needs a visible caveat; [X] is never used. No invented stats, quotes, customers or testimonials.
  • Read before writing: top and bottom rated assets for the industry, plus the feedback log.
  • File after finishing: under the requester, in the right industry folder.
  • Humans approve: options with a short rationale first; nothing is published or sent externally.
  • Real logos only: never redrawn, recoloured or generated by an image model.
  • Figma: team files are read-only; writes only into the output project (see Figma rules).

Weekly sync

A Claude scheduled task runs every Monday at 08:47 IST. It finds new and changed files with the Microsoft 365 connector, tags them with the six industries, uploads the new index to the dashboard, cleans up the previous one, and reports new files by industry plus the week's feedback digest. On the first Monday of a month it also removes files that no longer exist. It never moves, renames or deletes anything in OneDrive.

09 · Onboarding

Setup for a team member

About 20 minutes, most of it sign-in screens. Do the steps in order.

  1. Install the Claude desktop appDownload from claude.ai/download and sign in with your @leadsquared.com account. Check the workspace name in the account menu says Leadsquared INC, not Personal.
  2. Open the LSQ OS projectAsk Aditya for access if you can't see it. Start marketing requests inside this project so Claude has the product marketing context.
  3. Check the plugin is onSettings › Plugins (or Skills): leadsquared-marketing-os should show version 0.3.0 and be enabled. If it isn't there, ask an admin (see the next section). Restart the app after it appears.
  4. Connect the toolsSettings › Connectors: Figma (needed for Push to Figma and for studying templates), Microsoft 365 (Content Repository), and Higgsfield or Canva if you have seats. Make sure each is switched on for your chat.
  5. Open the Asset Library and joinOpen the dashboard link, accept the join prompt, and ask an Editor for Contributor so you can rate assets and save Studio designs.
  6. Try Template StudioCreate › pick a template › upload a speaker photo › Download PNG. Then try Push to Figma once and allow the page to use Figma when asked.
  7. Smoke test the agentsIn the LSQ OS project ask: "Write an on-brand headline for our Healthcare CRM page." Claude should use the voice skill and mention what it read from the library.

Checklist

  • Signed in to the Leadsquared INC workspace
  • LSQ OS project visible
  • Plugin leadsquared-marketing-os 0.3.0 enabled
  • Figma and Microsoft 365 connected and on
  • Joined the Asset Library with Contributor access
  • Made one PNG and one Figma push from the Studio
  • Smoke test passed
10 · Administration

Setup for an admin

Install or update the plugin

  1. Get leadsquared-marketing-os.plugin (version 0.3.0) from the dashboard's Get set up step, or from Documents › LSQ-OS-Outputs.
  2. claude.ai › Admin settings › Plugins (or Skills) › upload the file, make it available to the organisation and enabled by default.
  3. It replaces 0.2.1. Teammates pick it up when they restart the app.

Plugin descriptions must not contain angle brackets, and plain YAML descriptions must not contain a colon followed by a space; the uploader rejects both.

Dashboard access

  • The Asset Library is shared with the whole organisation. Sharing is changed from the page's Share menu.
  • New people join as Viewers. Editors and Owners approve them and promote them in Team.
  • Push to Figma uses each viewer's own Figma connector; nobody shares tokens.

Scheduled task

LSQ Content Repository weekly sync, Mondays 08:47 IST, automatic approval. Manage it from Claude's scheduled tasks list. If the OneDrive repository moves to a team site, update the drive and folder IDs in its prompt.

Skill hygiene

Never leave two skills covering the same ground enabled at once; which one fires is not predictable. Two known overlaps are listed under Known limits.

11 · Recipes

Everyday workflows

Make a webinar banner for LinkedIn
  1. Asset Library › Create › filter by industry › open the template.
  2. Type the title, date, time and button text. Add speakers: photo, name, designation, company logo.
  3. Check the Team feedback Claude applies panel for anything the team asked for on this template.
  4. Download PNG at 2x, or Push to Figma if a designer will polish it. Save to library so it can be rated.
Get a V2 or a brand-new template
  1. Open the closest template, choose Make a V2 or Totally new template, fill the content and describe the change.
  2. Copy the brief and paste it in a chat in the LSQ OS project. Attach the speaker photos and logos.
  3. Claude studies the source frame (read only), applies the team feedback, and returns 2–3 options. Pick one; it is added to the Figma output project and filed in the library.
Run a paid campaign

In the LSQ OS project: "Plan a LinkedIn and Google Search campaign for Education admissions, goal demo bookings, landing page …". The PPC agent picks three angles from ratings and personas, writes copy, makes creatives, runs the brand review, and files each piece with its channel and angle so ratings feed the next campaign.

Find existing material

Content Repository view in the dashboard, or ask Claude: "Find the latest healthcare case studies and the ELEVATE 2026 deck." The repository agent searches the index and opens files through Microsoft 365.

Rate and teach

Open an asset card, pick 1–5 stars, write what worked or what to change, and submit. Re-rating later adds a new comment; it doesn't erase the old one.

Adding a template

  1. Give Claude the Figma frame link. It studies the frame read-only and writes a module in src/studio/templates/ with fields, speakers, CSS and HTML at the exact frame size.
  2. Backgrounds go in as image files so the PNG and the Figma push match the preview.
  3. Register it in templates/index.js, run the render test, compare against the Figma screenshot, publish, and add it to the plugin's template catalogue.
13 · When something goes wrong

Troubleshooting

Push to Figma says Figma isn't connected or needs renewing

Connect or reconnect Figma in claude.ai Settings › Connectors, then click Push to Figma again and allow the page to use it.

Push to Figma button is greyed out

The page can't reach Figma for you in this view, usually because you opened a public or outside link. Open the dashboard while signed in to the Leadsquared workspace.

The PNG didn't download

Your browser blocked the save. The image opens on the page: right-click it and choose Save image as. On a phone, press and hold.

"Couldn't remove the background"

Very old browsers can't run the cut-out model, and some photos have no clear person. Try a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari, or upload a photo with one person and a plain background. You can also upload a PNG that is already cut out.

The cut-out has a light edge

Busy or light backgrounds close to the hair colour are the hardest. Use a photo with more contrast, or ask a designer to touch up the mask in Figma after Push to Figma (the mask is its own layer).

I can't rate or save to the library

You're a Viewer. Ask an Editor for Contributor in Team.

Claude isn't using the brand voice or the agents

The plugin is off, the app wasn't restarted after an update, or you're in a Personal workspace. Check the workspace name, enable the plugin, and start a new chat in the LSQ OS project.

Claude can't see Figma or SharePoint

The connector is authenticated but switched off for that chat. Turn it on in the chat's connector menu.

Repository counts look stale

The index refreshes on Mondays. "Last synced" on the Content Repository view shows the date.
